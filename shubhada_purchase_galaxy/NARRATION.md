# Video narration — "Your purchase order screen, in Odoo"

**Runtime:** ~3 minutes · **For:** Mahesh Joshi, Shubhada Polymers
**Rule for the recording:** let each screen settle before the next line starts. Silence is fine.

---

### 1 · Open — 0:00

**SCREEN** — Mahesh's Galaxy purchase order screenshot, then cut to the Odoo menu
**Purchase (Shubhada) → Purchase Orders**

> Mahesh, on our call you put your purchase order screen on the shared screen and walked me through it. You said Odoo doesn't have segmentation, doesn't have your series permissions, doesn't have your numbering, and doesn't have your maker-checker.
>
> So here's that screen. In Odoo. This took a day.

---

### 2 · The series — 0:20

**SCREEN** — Configuration → Purchase Series (all six rows visible)

> These are your six series. Bought-out, subcontracting, local capital, open order, subcontracting with bill of material, and service.
>
> Each one keeps its own running serial — look at the counters, they're all different numbers. And each one is tied to its own buyer group. A bought-out buyer opens this list and simply doesn't see a capital order. Not hidden with a filter he can clear. It isn't in his data.

---

### 3 · Raising the order — 0:50

**SCREEN** — new order, pick series PC01, division NSK, party S.S TRADERS, five items load

> Now let's raise one. Same series you showed me, PC01, bought-out material. Nashik division. S.S Traders. And the same five items off your screen — hand sleeves, masking tape, head caps, nose masks, cotton roll.

**SCREEN** — point at the Last Purchased Rate column

> This column is the last rate you actually paid this vendor for this item. It's sitting there while the buyer types the new one, so he's comparing before he commits, not after.

---

### 4 · Schedule and PR — 1:20

**SCREEN** — open line 1 → Delivery Schedule grid, then the Document Reference tab

> Open a line and there's your delivery schedule underneath it. Required date, required quantity, received, rejected, short-closed, cancelled — per line, not per order.
>
> And here's the PR this order answers. SHN27A001269, raised by Stores.

---

### 5 · The number — the key moment — 1:45

**SCREEN** — save the order. Stay on the empty Number field for a beat.

> Now I'll save it.
>
> And look at the top. **There's no number.** That's deliberate. You made a point of this — in Galaxy the document isn't real until somebody approves it, so it doesn't get a number until then. Same here.

**SCREEN** — Submit for Approval → switch user → HOD Approve

> Submit for approval. Now I'll switch to the HOD and approve it.
>
> One thing worth saying out loud: if I try to approve my own order, it stops me. The person who raised it can't be the person who cleared it.

**SCREEN** — Post. Let the number appear. Hold on it.

> And post.
>
> **SHN27PO04204.**
>
> Yours was oh-four-two-oh-three. This is the next one. SH for Shubhada Polymers, N for Nashik, 27 for this financial year, PO for the document, and the running serial off that series — same as your system builds it.

---

### 6 · Amendment — 2:25

**SCREEN** — press Amend, show the amendment number and date appear, status flips to Amended

> Amendments. Press amend, and the amendment number goes to 1, the date is stamped, the status turns to Amended, and the order goes straight back into the approval chain. Nobody quietly edits a posted order.

---

### 7 · The audit tab — 2:45

**SCREEN** — Audit tab: Created / Modified / HOD approved / Posted

> And the audit. Created by, modified by, who approved it, who posted it — with the time, to the second.
>
> Every one of those was written by the system. None of them can be typed.

---

### 8 · The honest close — 3:00

**SCREEN** — back to the posted order, wide

> Now the part I'm not going to dress up.
>
> This is the design. It is **not tested.** You told me on the call that testing is 60% of the work, and you were right — that's the real conversation, not whether we can build it fast.
>
> But you also said the scope definition would be a very long thing. So here's one screen off your own system, rebuilt in a day, for you to poke holes in. Tell me what's wrong with it and I'll fix it while we're still talking.

## Recording notes

- **Two logins needed** — one to raise and submit, a second (HOD/Plant Head) to approve and post. Have both windows open before you start.
- Don't rush section 5. The empty Number field and then **SHN27PO04204** appearing is the whole video.
- Zoom the browser to about 110% so the field labels are readable when he watches it on a phone.
- If a screen is slow, stop talking and let it load. Dead air reads as confidence.
